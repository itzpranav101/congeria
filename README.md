# Congeria

A heist game made at Hack Club Haven Singapore (theme: BOUNCE BACK).

Harry Baals, barista and amateur thief, has $67 and wants money, gold, diamonds and GTA 6. Rosie Cheeks is a rookie cop on her first shift. Ben Dover is a streamer whose chat decides whose side he's on. Knock out your rival and you swap lives.

Play: https://itzpranav101.github.io/congeria/

## The heist
1. Lift the staff keycard off the cleaner's cart in the lobby
2. Kill the laser grid at the bottom of the vault stairs
3. Deal with the vault guard: knife him (loud) or knock him out while his back is turned
4. Crack the vault dial by ear
5. Survive the lockdown: rewire the fuse box or escape through the bunker tunnel
6. Saw the cage padlock, bag the cash, gold and diamonds
7. Stand on your spot at Skibidi Coffee for 3 seconds

## Rules
- Weapons unlock after your first task (Harry: the keycard, Rosie: her first scene, Ben: meeting his partner)
- Past the lobby is trespassing: Rosie can attack you there
- Rosie can only arrest Harry if he's carrying the loot
- Get knocked out and you bounce back into the life of whoever beat you
- Ben never swaps: if he knocks you out, you lose

## Controls
WASD move · mouse look · click attack (Harry's knife, Rosie's rifle) · E use · Q special · 1 weapon out/away · 2 coffee bomb (Harry) · R jam Rosie's radio · Space dodge · Shift run · Tab map
